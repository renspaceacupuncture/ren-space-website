About Lauren

Hi, I'm Lauren, a licensed acupuncturist practising in Walthamstow and Dalston, dedicated to helping you through personalised, compassionate care.

I have a particular interest in supporting a wide range of long-term conditions, menstrual, hormonal and pelvic health, as well as pain - including musculoskeletal issues - stress, anxiety & sleep.

My clinical approach is grounded in Traditional Chinese Medicine, and shaped by the belief that no two people's experiences are the same. My aim is to create a calm, kind and non-judgemental space where you feel heard, understood and supported.

I hold a Licentiate in Acupuncture (Lic.Ac) and am a fully insured member of the British Acupuncture Council.
